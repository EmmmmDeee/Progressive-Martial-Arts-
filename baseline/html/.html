THE PMAAI SYSTEM

PMAAI teaches a progressive system that employs the most effective techniques from a spectrum of arts to create a well-rounded martial artist who can handle diverse situations. The current popularity of MMA and its culture proves with each and every fight the legitimacy of having a broad fighting curriculum. 

Our instructors have been in the industry for years and draw from world-renowned sources of knowledge. Peruse our website to find out more about the arts we teach.

MINI MUSCLES – HEALTHY ACTIVE KIDS

Mini Muscles offers a fun yet disciplined environment for your children to develop self-defence skills, strength, motor control, balance and coordination. Our lessons focus around martial arts, BEASTFIT Kids & Fit Play. Lessons are structured to help with learning new things, however, these skills are also guided discovery- and play-orientated to allow your child to develop a broad array of physical attributes and self-expression.

The martial arts component is focused on self-defence and draws from our Muay Thai Kickboxing, Combat Submission Wrestling, Brazilian Jiu Jitsu and basic self-defence. The grappling teaches the children escapes from holds and grabs and how to control someone who is trying to strike them.
